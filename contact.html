FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

Do I need experience to join an expedition? +

It depends on the route. Beginner-friendly treks require only basic fitness, while high-altitude expeditions require prior climbing experience, technical skills, and proper acclimatization. Always check the requirements of each expedition.

What equipment do I need? +

Essential gear includes boots, crampons, an ice axe, a helmet, a harness, layers of clothing, and a first-aid kit. For high-altitude climbs, you'll also need a down suit, insulated gloves, and a four-season tent.

How long does acclimatization take? +

Above 3,000 m, most climbers acclimatize at a rate of 300–500 m per day, with a rest day every 1,000 m. High-altitude expeditions often take 4–8 weeks to properly acclimatize.

Is mountaineering dangerous? +

Yes — mountaineering carries real risks including falls, avalanches, altitude sickness, and weather. Proper training, qualified guides, and honest judgment reduce these risks significantly.

Can you help me train for a climb? +

Yes. We can recommend training programs based on your goals, fitness level, and target mountain. Contact us with your plans and we'll guide you.

Do you organize custom expeditions? +

Absolutely. Tell us your target peak, dates, and experience level, and we'll connect you with the right guides and logistics.
