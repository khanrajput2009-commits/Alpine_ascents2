🏔️ Mountain Rules

  • Always stay on marked trails to protect fragile alpine flora.
  • Carry out everything you carry in (Leave No Trace).
  • Respect local wildlife. Do not feed animals.
  • Weather changes rapidly. Always follow your guide's instructions.
  • Keep a minimum distance of 5 meters from glaciers.

💡 Pro Tips for Climbers

  • Acclimatize: Spend a day in the village before ascending.
  • Hydrate: Drink twice as much water as you do at sea level.
  • Layer Up: Wear moisture-wicking layers. Avoid cotton.
  • Start Early: Begin ascents at 4 AM to avoid afternoon storms.
  • Pack Light: Only carry what is absolutely necessary.
